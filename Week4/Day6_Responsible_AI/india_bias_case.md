# AI Bias Case Study in India

One example of AI bias in India involves facial recognition systems used for identity verification. Studies have shown that these systems often perform less accurately for people with darker skin tones compared to lighter skin tones. This bias mainly results from representation bias, where the training datasets contain fewer examples of diverse skin tones.

The lower accuracy can lead to authentication failures, inconvenience, and unequal treatment for affected users. To reduce this bias, developers should train models using more diverse datasets, continuously evaluate performance across demographic groups, and perform regular fairness audits.

Responsible AI practices such as transparency, fairness testing, and model documentation help reduce these risks and improve trust in AI systems.
