Artificial intelligence in healthcare can support diagnosis,
medical image analysis, drug discovery, and patient monitoring.

AI can analyze medical images such as X-rays, CT scans,
and MRI scans to help identify possible abnormalities.

Natural language processing helps healthcare systems
extract useful information from medical records and reports.

AI is designed to support healthcare professionals.
Doctors and other medical professionals remain responsible
for clinical decisions.

Challenges of AI in healthcare include data privacy,
bias, limited data quality, security risks, and
the need for human oversight.
