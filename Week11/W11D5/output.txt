Question: What are the benefits of AI in healthcare?
Answer: The benefits of AI in healthcare include: 

1. Supporting diagnosis
2. Analyzing medical images
3. Assisting in drug discovery
4. Monitoring patients

Question: How is AI used in medical image analysis?
Answer: AI is used in medical image analysis, such as analyzing X-rays, CT scans, and MRI scans to help identify possible abnormalities.

Question: What is the role of NLP in healthcare?
Answer: According to the provided context, Natural Language Processing (NLP) helps healthcare systems extract useful information from medical records and reports.

Question: Does AI replace healthcare professionals?
Answer: No, AI does not replace healthcare professionals. Doctors and other medical professionals remain responsible for clinical decisions.

Question: What are the challenges of AI in healthcare?
Answer: The challenges of AI in healthcare include data privacy, bias, limited data quality, security risks, and the need for human oversight.
