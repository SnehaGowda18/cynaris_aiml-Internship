
Artificial intelligence in healthcare can improve diagnosis, treatment planning,
patient monitoring, medical research, and administrative work.

AI is used in medical image analysis to identify patterns in X-rays, CT scans,
MRI scans, ultrasound images, and pathology slides. It can support doctors by
detecting possible abnormalities.

Natural language processing in healthcare can process clinical notes, medical
records, discharge summaries, research papers, and patient messages.

AI does not completely replace healthcare professionals. Doctors and nurses
provide clinical judgment, empathy, ethical decisions, communication, and
responsibility for patient care.

Benefits of AI in healthcare include faster analysis, improved workflow,
early detection of disease, personalized treatment, and reduced administrative
work.

Challenges of healthcare AI include privacy risks, biased training data,
lack of explainability, security threats, incorrect predictions, and the need
for human supervision.

Healthcare AI systems must protect patient information through access control,
encryption, secure storage, and privacy-preserving practices.

Machine learning models require representative and high-quality healthcare
data. Poor-quality or biased data can produce unreliable results.

AI can support drug discovery by analyzing biological data, predicting
molecular properties, and identifying possible drug candidates.

Remote patient monitoring systems can use AI to analyze vital signs and
identify unusual patterns that may require medical attention.
