Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by computers.

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data.

Deep Learning uses neural networks with multiple hidden layers.

Python is one of the most popular programming languages for AI because of its simple syntax and rich libraries.

ChromaDB is a vector database used to store embeddings for semantic search.

Ollama allows users to run large language models locally.

Local LLMs provide better privacy because data never leaves your computer.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines document retrieval with language models to answer questions.
