Retrieval-Augmented Generation, or RAG, is a technique used in 
artificial intelligence applications. 
 
RAG combines information retrieval with language generation. First, 
documents are divided into smaller chunks. These chunks are 
converted into numerical embeddings and stored in a vector database. 
 
ChromaDB is a vector database that can store embeddings and retrieve 
information based on semantic similarity. 
 
Cosine similarity is commonly used to compare embeddings.
